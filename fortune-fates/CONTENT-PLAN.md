## 1. The goal: be honest about what "100k in month 1" means

No one can guarantee a video goes viral. A new channel has no history, so YouTube has to test every video on small audiences first. What we *can* do is stack every factor that worked for So Sánh Nhanh:

- Their **fate videos** had a median of **~300k views**, versus 13k for their other topics (16 videos).
- About **1 in 4** of all their videos passed 100k.

**Targets for month 1:**

| Target | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Main goal** | **100k total channel views** in 30 days |
| Stretch goal | One video passes 100k on its own |
| Uploads | 14–16 long videos (3–4 a week) and 2–3 Shorts per long video |

**How the math gets to 100k:** 16 videos × average 3–5k = 50–80k, plus one breakout video at 30–100k = **100k+**. You need volume, because you can't predict which video will break out. So Sánh Nhanh's hits were scattered among many uploads.

## 2. The repeatable formula: "FATE CARDS"

### Step 1: Topic test (an idea must pass at least 4 of 5)
1. **Famous:** viewers already know the names or brands (CEOs, billionaires, brands, games, athletes).
2. **Fate:** every entry has a story arc: rise, fall, caught, fired, comeback, or broke.
3. **Number:** every card has a hard **$ figure or time figure** (before → after).
4. **Timing:** it's tied to news from the last 1–3 days, or to a yearly search spike (Black Friday, tax season, year-end).
5. **"Who's missing?":** the list has 10+ entries and people will argue about what was left out. That drives comments and gives you Part 2.

### Step 2: Title templates
- `From [Famous A] to [Famous B]: [Group] Who [Fate]`
  e.g. *From Steve Jobs to Jensen Huang: CEOs Who Got Fired… Then Came Back*
- `[Group] Who [Fate] ([Number A] vs [Number B])`
  e.g. *Fraudsters Who Got Caught (Amount Stolen vs Years in Prison)*
- `[Thing] Then vs Now: [Number] → [Number]`
  e.g. *Startups Worth Billions That Went to $0*
- Rules: put names in the first 40 characters, add one number, and use at most one CAPS word (FIRED, BROKE, $0).

### Step 3: Thumbnail formula (see `/thumbnails`)
- 4 cards in a row, each with photo, name bar, short reason and **one huge number**.
- **The last card is highlighted** with a yellow border. It holds the newest story or the most shocking number.
- Use one curiosity device per thumbnail: a "?" card, a "+31%" chip, a red "BANKRUPT" stamp, or green/red numbers.
- If faces aren't famous (lottery winners), use silhouettes and let the **money number** be the hook.
- Test at phone size: if you can't read the number at 1/4 size, make it bigger.

### Step 4: Video structure (4–6 minutes at first, 8+ minutes later)
| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | **Hook:** flash the last, most shocking card for 1 second, plus one line: "Number 1 lost $113 billion in 48 hours." |
| 0:05–0:15 | One sentence of context (why now: "Nvidia just brought back its CEO…") |
| Cards | Order them **from least to most shocking**, 8–15 seconds each, with year, name, fate line and the number animating up |
| Last card | The news hook or biggest number. This is the payoff you promised in the hook. |
| End | "Who did I miss? Comment below → Part 2", then an end screen pointing to a related video |

- **8:00 minutes and up** turns on ads in the middle of the video, which is the biggest earnings lever. Move there once viewers watch at least 40% of a 5-minute video.
- Add your own value so YouTube doesn't treat the videos as reused content: a short voiceover or on-screen narration line per card, and a sources list in the description.

### Step 5: After publishing
- **Pin a comment:** "Who should be in Part 2? 👇"
- **If a video beats your channel median by 3× within 72 hours:** make **Part 2 within 7 days**, then an **"UPDATED 2027"** version later. So Sánh Nhanh's jail video got 600k twice this way.
- Cut **2–3 Shorts** from each video (the top-card reveal) to bring in subscribers. Shorts pay little, so treat them as support only.

## 3. Six content pillars (fate formula across money, tech and more)

Rotate between them so the channel isn't one note, but every pillar keeps the formula: **famous + fate + number**.

### 💰 A. Billionaires & money swings
- Biggest one-day fortune gains & losses ever
- Billionaires 2016 vs 2026: who rose, who fell (fits the "10-year challenge" trend)
- Richest person in every US state *(easy to relate to)*
- Billionaires who lost it all (peak → now)
- How old each billionaire was when they made their first $1M

### 💻 B. Tech & AI
- Startups worth billions that went to $0 (Theranos, FTX, WeWork, Quibi, Juicero, Byju's, Builder.ai…)
- CEOs fired then came back (Jobs, Iger, Altman, Huang)
- Tech products that flopped and how much they lost (Google Glass, Quibi, Humane AI Pin…). Post around **CES, Jan 6–9**.
- New AI billionaires: how fast they got rich (DeepSeek founder now $55B…)
- Giants that missed the future (Nokia, BlackBerry, Kodak, Yahoo): peak value → now

### 🏬 C. Brands & companies
- Black Friday ghosts: bankrupt stores (Sears, Toys"R"Us, Circuit City, Blockbuster…)
- Companies that died in 2026 *(publish at year-end)*
- Brands that came back from bankruptcy (Marvel, GM, Delta, Six Flags…)
- Most valuable company each year, 1990–2026

### 🎮 D. Gaming & entertainment money
- Biggest launches in history: day-one $ (GTA 6 passed an estimated $1.6B; GTA V took $800M on day one)
- Video games that cost millions and died fast (Concord, Anthem, Babylon's Fall…)
- Movies that lost the most money ever
- Child stars: earnings then vs now

### ⚖️ E. White-collar crime
- Fraudsters: amount stolen vs years in prison (Madoff, SBF, Holmes, Stanford, Do Kwon…)
- Celebrities jailed for money crimes (Martha Stewart, Wesley Snipes, Lauryn Hill, the Chrisleys…). **Peak timing: tax season, Jan–Apr.**
- CEOs brought down by affairs or scandals
- Pardoned: rich people who walked free
- Still missing: fugitives with stolen billions (Ruja Ignatova "?" card)

### 🏆 F. Sports money & relatable money
- Athletes who earned $100M+ and went broke (Tyson, Antoine Walker, Vince Young…)
- Lottery winners who went broke
- What a $100K salary is really worth in every state
- Minimum wage in every country / how long it takes to become a millionaire in each country

**Ad-safety rule for all pillars:** use money words (*bankrupt, fired, net worth, fraud, prison*). Avoid suicide, drugs or gore on cards and thumbnails, because those get limited ads.

## 5. Rapid-response playbook (how to catch news within 24 hours)

The fastest channel wins news traffic. Prepare these **ready-made card lists** now (researched, photos collected) so you only add the newest card when news breaks:

1. CEOs fired / resigned / came back
2. Biggest bankruptcies
3. Biggest frauds (stolen vs sentence)
4. Biggest one-day fortune swings
5. Startups that went to $0
6. Celebrity / athlete bankruptcies

**When news breaks:** add the new card as the **last, highlighted card**, put the new name in the title ("…RIP / FIRED / ARRESTED: [Name]"), use the thumbnail template, and publish. So Sánh Nhanh's #1 video worked exactly like this ("RIP Jayden Adams" added to an existing series).

## 6. Weekly loop and metrics

**Weekly routine:**
- **Mon:** check news and Google Trends, then pick this week's 3–4 topics using the 5-point test.
- **Tue–Sat:** research, then build cards, then make the thumbnail with `thumbnail-maker`, then publish.
- **Sun:** review stats. Choose a Part 2 or remake, and write down what worked.

| Metric | Healthy level for a new channel | If it's low |
|---|---|---|
| Click-through rate (first 48h) | 5–10% | Change the thumbnail (bigger number, better last card) |
| Average % viewed (4–6 min video) | 40–55% | Stronger hook, faster cards, save the best card for last |
| Comments per 1,000 views | 2+ | Ask "Who did I miss?" more clearly and pin it |
| A video beats your median by 3× | — | Make Part 2 within 7 days |
